Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

1. What We Collect

When you use PaperPulse, we collect:

  • Your email address and name (via Google Sign-In)
  • Topics you select during onboarding
  • Papers you view, bookmark, and highlight
  • Your digest schedule preference
  • Reading streak data
  • Device information necessary for push notifications

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Personalise your weekly paper digest
  • Deliver push notifications on your chosen schedule
  • Sync your bookmarks and highlights across sessions
  • Display your reading streak and stats

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

PaperPulse uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In — authentication (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase — database and file storage, hosted on AWS (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Anthropic Claude API — used to generate paper summaries from paper text only; no user data is sent
  • arXiv API / Semantic Scholar API — paper metadata retrieval; no user data is sent

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. As this is a beta, data may be reset between major versions. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing us. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Children

PaperPulse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
