Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

1. Summary

PaperPulse is a local-first reading app. In the beta, we do not create a cloud account for you, we do not ask for your email or name, and we do not sync your reading to any server. Everything you do in the app (the papers you read, bookmark, highlight, and the topics you pick) stays on your device.

2. What is Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following information locally, on your phone only:

  • A random device identifier so your data is namespaced on this device
  • The topics you select during onboarding
  • Papers you view, bookmark, and highlight
  • Your digest schedule preference
  • Reading streak data
  • Cached paper metadata so the app works offline

None of this information is transmitted to us or to any server we control.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • No email address, name, or profile information
  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reports that include personal data

4. Third-Party Services

PaperPulse fetches paper metadata from public academic APIs. These requests contain the paper IDs and search terms needed to return results. They do not contain any information that identifies you personally.

  • arXiv API: public paper metadata and abstracts
  • HuggingFace Daily Papers API: curated daily list of notable papers

Notifications are delivered locally on your device using Android's built-in notification system and WorkManager; no push token is sent to a remote server.

5. Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet: to fetch paper metadata from arXiv and HuggingFace Daily Papers
  • Notifications: to remind you when your digest is ready (optional, you can decline)

6. Deleting Your Data

Because your data lives only on your device, you have full control:

  • In-app: open Profile → Account → Delete all my data. This wipes every bookmark, highlight, interest, schedule, cache, and the device identifier from the app's storage. The change is instant and cannot be undone.
  • Uninstall: removing the app from your phone also deletes everything the app stored.
  • Email: if you need help or want to request deletion in writing, email aryasundev@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

PaperPulse is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. If we introduce any feature that changes what is collected or where it is stored, we will update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
