Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models

Wei et al. · Google Research

Generating a chain of thought, a series of intermediate reasoning steps, significantly improves how large language models handle complex reasoning. Prompting a 540B-parameter model with just eight chain-of-thought exemplars hits state-of-the-art accuracy on GSM8K math problems, beating fine-tuned GPT-3 with a verifier.
